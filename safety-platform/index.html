THE SOLUTION

Designed for the moment help is needed.

The original Five Stones Safety website presents the CENTEGIX Safety Platform as a comprehensive approach to alerting and response, beyond a single device.

01

Wearable activation

Give staff a dedicated way to request assistance without needing to locate a mobile phone or open an app.

02

Dynamic digital mapping

Support awareness of the location where an alert is initiated so responders can make more informed decisions.

03

Notification and coordination

Provide school leadership and response teams with an integrated picture of the situation.
