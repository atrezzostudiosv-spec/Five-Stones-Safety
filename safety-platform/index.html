FROM ALERT TO RESPONSE

Designed for the moment help is needed.

CENTEGIX CrisisAlert connects wearable activation, location information and coordinated notifications as part of a comprehensive safety platform.

MAKE AN INFORMED DECISION

Compare alert technologies.

Different silent panic alarm technologies have distinct trade-offs. Evaluate the fit for your campuses, staff and response protocols.

Advantages and limitations of common SPAT approaches
TechnologyAdvantagesConsiderations
WearablesSimple push-button activation; portable and durable; dedicated device managed by the district.Staff must wear the badge; some models can be bulky.
Phone appsCan be economical; phones are widely available.Dependent on cellular connectivity; personal-device and privacy concerns; phone must be carried; locating and operating the app under stress; updates required.
Fixed panic buttonsDedicated hardware with continuous power.May be more costly and require installation work; not mobile; typically indoor-only coverage; often a single alert type.
