MAKE AN INFORMED DECISION

Compare alert technologies.

Evaluate the trade-offs between wearable, app-based and stationary silent panic alarm technology (SPAT). Select a solution that fits the needs of your staff and campuses.

Wearables

Advantages

Simple push-button activation; portable and durable; dedicated device managed by the district.

Considerations

Staff must wear the badge; some models can be bulky.

Phone apps

Advantages

Can be economical; phones are widely available.

Considerations

Dependent on cellular connectivity; personal-device and privacy concerns; phone must be carried; locating and operating the app under stress; updates required.

Fixed panic buttons

Advantages

Dedicated hardware with continuous power.

Considerations

May be more costly and require installation work; not mobile; typically indoor-only coverage; often a single alert type.
